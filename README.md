# PROG101_Assignment_FavourKoroma_905006562.
:Assignment 1 for Programming Logic and Design: the logical design of a school attendance tracking system for Sierra Leone. The system records children's daily attendance (present, absent or late), calculates attendance rates, flags pupils who are often absent and produces reports for teachers and head teachers.

##The problem
Many schools in Sierra Leone record attendance on paper. Totals are added by hand, errors are common, and pupils who are often absent are noticed too late. This project designs the logic of a system that records daily attendance, calculates attendance rates, flags pupils at risk (below 75%), notifies guardians and reports to head teachers.

##The solutions 
Develop a Student Attendance Management System that replaces the traditional paper-based method of recording attendance with a digital platform. The system will allow lecturers to record student attendance, manage attendance records, and monitor students' participation in classes. It will also help reduce errors, prevent the loss of attendance information, and make it easier to identify students with frequent absences. By providing accurate records and simplifying attendance management, the system will save time, improve efficiency, and support better academic performance.

##SDG 4: Quality Education
The Student Attendance System supports SDG 4 by improving attendance tracking, encouraging student participation, and promoting accountability in schools. It helps lecturers identify students with frequent absences, maintain accurate attendance records, and provide support where needed. By reducing manual errors and saving time, the system contributes to a better learning environment and improved academic performance.
